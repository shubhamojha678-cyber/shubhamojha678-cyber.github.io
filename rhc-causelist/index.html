Questions about the Rajasthan High Court cause list

When is the Rajasthan High Court cause list published?

The Daily cause list for a hearing day is normally published on the court’s website the evening before. Supplementary lists can follow later, including on the hearing day itself. The timing varies from day to day, so this page shows the time at which each list was last fetched.

What is a supplementary cause list?

A Supplementary cause list is an additional list published after the Daily list has been prepared, with matters added later for the same hearing day. A case can appear in the Daily list, in a Supplementary list, or in both. On this site the badges D, S and D+S show which list an entry comes from.

What does item number mean in a cause list?

The item number is the serial position of a case on a court’s board for that day. Courts generally take up matters in item order, but the order can change and a matter may not be reached. “With” before a number means the case is connected to that item.

How do I find my case in the cause list?

Type the case number (for example CW 4006/2011 or 4006/2011), the advocate’s name or a party’s name in the search box. Results show the seat, court number, bench, item number and list type. The search covers only the lists held here; a case that does not appear may still be on the court’s board, so confirm on hcraj.nic.in.

Jodhpur or Jaipur — which bench hears my case?

The Rajasthan High Court sits at its Principal Seat in Jodhpur and at a Bench in Jaipur, and each hears matters from the districts assigned to it. Each seat publishes its own cause list. The search here covers both seats together and shows the seat for every result.

Can I search the Rajasthan High Court cause list by CNR number?

Yes. Type the 16-character CNR (for example RJHC010541342024; Jodhpur numbers begin RJHC01, Jaipur Bench numbers RJHC02). If the case has appeared in any cause list we hold, the search shows its case number and every listing date in our database. For the case status and orders, use the Case Status page of hcraj.nic.in.
