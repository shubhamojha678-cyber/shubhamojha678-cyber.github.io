Source and official record

This site is a compilation of the Daily and Supplementary cause lists published by the Rajasthan High Court on hcraj.nic.in for its Principal Seat at Jodhpur and its Bench at Jaipur. It is not an official court service. The court’s own website is the only official record; if anything here differs from it, the court’s record prevails.

Not legal advice

The site shows public court information only. Nothing on it is legal advice or a solicitation.

Permitted use

You may use the search for personal, non-commercial lookup of a case, an advocate or a party.

Not permitted without written permission

Automated bulk download or scraping of this compilation, republishing it in whole or in substantial part, and framing or embedding this site inside another site or app are not permitted without the written permission of Shubham Ojha & Associates.

Attribution

Any quotation from this site must name the source: “Rajasthan High Court cause list search, compiled by Shubham Ojha & Associates (shubhamojhaandassociates.com/rhc-causelist/)”.

Privacy

Party names in sensitive matters (for example matters involving children, sexual offences, family and matrimonial disputes and habeas corpus) are withheld here; the case number, court and item stay searchable.

No warranty

The compilation is provided as it is, without any warranty. A list may be incomplete, delayed or out of date, and a case that does not appear here may still be on the court’s board — always confirm on hcraj.nic.in.
