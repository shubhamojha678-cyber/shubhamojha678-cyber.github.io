How listing works at the Rajasthan High Court

Unofficial compilation — not a website of the Rajasthan High Court; the court’s published cause list is the only official record. यह राजस्थान उच्च न्यायालय की आधिकारिक वेबसाइट नहीं है।

“When is my case listed?” means: on which date, in which court and at which serial number will the matter be called. The Rajasthan High Court answers that through its cause list. For every sitting day each seat — the Principal Seat at Jodhpur and the Bench at Jaipur — publishes a Daily cause list, normally on the evening before the hearing. Matters added later appear in a Supplementary list, which can come out on the hearing day itself.

A case therefore becomes visible only when the court has published the list for that date. Until then this page does not guess. Type the case number (the type, number and year, such as CW 1234/2026), the CNR, or the name of the advocate or a party in the box above. If the case is in a list we hold for today or a coming date, the answer card shows the date, the seat, the court number, the item number and whether it is in the Daily or the Supplementary list. The item number is the serial position on that court’s board; courts generally take matters in that order, though the order can change.

If the case is not in any list we hold yet, the card says so plainly, tells you up to which date lists are held, and shows the last date on which the case appeared. Tap ★ Save and the case stays in “My list” on your device; each time you open the page it is checked again against the newest lists. The date fixed by the court in its order sheet is part of the case status, which only the court’s own website shows — use Case Status on hcraj.nic.in for that, and always confirm a listing there.

Questions: when is my case listed?

When is my case listed in Rajasthan High Court Jodhpur?

Search your case number or CNR above. If the case is in a Daily or Supplementary cause list of the Principal Seat at Jodhpur that we hold for today or a coming date, you see the date, court number and item number. The Daily list for a day is normally published the evening before, so a case usually becomes visible one evening ahead.

How do I know the next date of my case?

The next date fixed by the court is recorded in the case status and the order sheet on hcraj.nic.in (Case Status). This page shows something different: the dates on which the case actually appears in a published cause list. Save the case with ★ and it shows in “My list” as soon as a list containing it is held here.

Mera case kab lagega? Rajasthan High Court mein case ki listing kaise dekhein?

Upar search box mein apna case number (jaise CW 1234/2026) ya CNR likhiye. Agar case aaj ya aane wali tareekh ki kisi cause list mein hai to tareekh, court number aur item number turant dikhega. Cause list aam taur par sunwai se ek shaam pehle jaari hoti hai. Kripya hcraj.nic.in par pushti zaroor karein.

Does this show the tentative or next date of my case?

No. Only actual listings are shown: a date appears here only when the case is printed in the Daily or Supplementary cause list published by the court for that date. Tentative or next dates shown in the court’s case status are not shown here. For the case status, use hcraj.nic.in.

How do I check a case listing date at the Jaipur Bench?

The same search covers the Jaipur Bench. Type the case number; if the same number exists at both seats the answer shows each separately, marked Jodhpur or Jaipur Bench. You can also choose “Jaipur only” under the search box.

My case does not appear in the cause list — what does that mean?

It means the case is not in the lists held here for the dates searched. The list for a later date may not have been published yet, a Supplementary list may still follow, or the matter may not be fixed for those days. A case that does not appear here may still be on the court’s board, so confirm on hcraj.nic.in.

Can I find my case by advocate name instead of case number?

Yes. Type the advocate’s name as it is printed in the cause list, for example M.S. PUROHIT. All matters with that name in the lists for today and the next hearing day are shown court by court, and the name can be saved with ★.
