Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

Shubham Ojha & Associates ("the Firm", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Information you provide

The contact form on this website prepares a message that you send to us yourself via WhatsApp or email. We receive only what you choose to send — typically your name, contact details, and a brief description of your matter. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and is treated as confidential.

Information collected automatically

We use a privacy-respecting web analytics service to understand how visitors use this website (pages viewed, approximate location at city level, device type, and referral source). Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking on this website.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies for advertising. Analytics may use minimal technical identifiers strictly for measuring visits. Your browser settings allow you to block these without affecting your ability to use the site.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information you share in a legal enquiry is protected by professional confidentiality obligations applicable to advocates. We may disclose information only where required by law.

Data protection

We handle personal data in keeping with the principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may write to us at shubham@shubhamojhaandassociates.com to ask what information we hold about you or to request its correction or deletion.

Third-party links

This website links to external resources (court portals, government services). Their privacy practices are their own; please review their policies separately.

Contact

For any privacy-related question, contact us at shubham@shubhamojhaandassociates.com or +91 70230 51275.
