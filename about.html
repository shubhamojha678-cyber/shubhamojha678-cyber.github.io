About Advocate Shubham Ojha

Shubham Ojha is an Advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Rajasthan, practising before the Rajasthan High Court, Jodhpur Bench, and the District & Sessions Courts at Jodhpur. The chamber, Shubham Ojha & Associates, advises and represents clients across property, family, civil, criminal, constitutional and service-law matters.

The practice combines careful statutory and precedent-based analysis with clear, plain-language guidance, so that clients understand both the law that governs their matter and the realistic course it is likely to take. Particular attention is given to Rajasthan-specific procedure — the division of jurisdiction between civil and revenue courts, the Rajasthan Tenancy Act, 1955, land-record and mutation practice, and proceedings before the Board of Revenue.

Approach

Every matter begins with an honest assessment of its merits, the appropriate forum, the documents required, and the likely timeline and cost — before any step is taken. Where a fair settlement or family arrangement serves the client better than prolonged litigation, that route is placed on the table first. Where litigation is necessary, the matter is pleaded and conducted with diligence and full disclosure to the client at each stage.

The firm also publishes plain-language legal explainers in English and Hindi so that members of the public can understand their rights and the procedure that applies to common disputes. These writings are for general information and awareness; they are not advertisements and do not solicit work.
